Take a few slow breaths when the phone break starts

A break rule stops the scrolling, then leaves you looking at a timer. Many people spend those minutes waiting to get back in. Kids often spend them asking a parent for the code. I make Ripple, a free app blocker with no account. Since Android v0.1.23, the break screen has a way to listen to sleep sounds or do slow breathing while the timer runs. Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5 and does not have this.

What is on the break screen

Where it shows up

The single app "Take a break" screen and the whole phone eye rest screen show "Put the phone down and relax for a bit", with "Sleep sounds" and "Breathe".

The timer keeps going

The break counts down as usual. The relax page has no button that goes back to the restricted app.

It ends with the break

Sleep sounds opened from a break stop when the break ends and fade out over the last 30 seconds. Slow breathing sets the number of rounds from the time left.

Sounds for breathing

Breath tide, singing bowl, slow heartbeat, low hum and wind chimes, or rain, waves and the other sleep sounds, or silence. Tap one to hear 5 seconds first.

On your own phone

Give short video apps a break rule

Give the app you lose the most time in its own "Take a Break" rule (in Daily limits, tap the app, then Take a Break). When the break starts, tap Breathe and do the rounds until the timer ends.

Use it at night too

The Schedules page has a "Sleep sounds & slow breathing" card at the top, so you can open them before bed without waiting for a break.

Keep the wait honest

Loosening a rule needs a wait (15 minutes by default, can be set to 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours) or the unlock code. Strict mode leaves only the code.

iPhone

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps on a schedule through Screen Time, with uninstall protection, protection against clock changes and an unlock code. There is no break screen, sleep sounds or breathing on iPhone.

On a child's phone

Something to do while waiting

A kid who hits a break on a tablet or phone can listen to rain or follow the breathing circle instead of sitting with a countdown.

The code stays with you

Set the rules and the unlock code on the child's device yourself and keep the code. The relax page cannot reopen the blocked app.

Apps that open a study app

If an app is set to "Open After Limit", the break opens the chosen study app, and that break screen does not show the relax entry.

What Ripple cannot do

There is no remote management. You set everything on the child's phone and cannot see or change it from your own phone.

Setting it up

Update

Install Android v0.1.23 or later over your current version. Your rules stay.

Add a break rule

In Daily limits tap an app, set a daily limit, then tap Take a Break and choose how long to use and how long to rest.

Pick a sound

Open the card on the Schedules page, tap Breathe, and preview the sounds until one feels right.

Try one break

Let the break happen once and tap Breathe or Sleep sounds on the break screen.

FAQ

Has this been tested on a real phone?

The relax entry on break screens was checked on an emulator and in unit tests. A full break has not been run through on a real phone yet. Long sleep sound playback with the screen off, incoming calls and unplugging headphones have not been tested on a real phone either.

Can my kid use the relax page to get back into the game?

No. The relax page has no way back to the restricted app, and the break keeps counting down.

Does the iPhone version have breathing?

No. iPhone v0.1.5 only blocks on a schedule, with uninstall protection, clock change protection and the unlock code.

Does it cost anything?

No. Ripple is free, has no ads and no in-app payments.
