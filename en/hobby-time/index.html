Your phone keeps interrupting drawing, crafts and handwriting practice, and kids want the tablet during coloring and crafts

After work you want to draw for a while, fill a few pages of handwriting practice or build a model. You set everything out, pick up the phone out of habit, and half an hour later the page is still blank. On the weekend your child starts coloring, play dough or a craft and a few minutes in asks to play on the tablet. This page covers adults setting this up for themselves first, then parents setting it up for their kids. Android is on v0.1.20 and iPhone on v0.1.5. They have different features, so they are covered separately.

Adults: lock the apps you scroll before you sit down

Turn on Quick Focus before you start

On Android open Quick Focus, choose lock only the apps I select, add short video, social and shopping apps, and set it for about how long you plan to draw, such as 1 hour. It lifts when the time is up.

Keep the apps you need off the list

If you follow a tutorial, look at reference photos, use a handwriting app or photograph your work, leave those apps off the list. If the tutorial lives in a short video app, save that one video somewhere else first, then lock the short video app.

For regular practice, add a schedule

Schedules can be set by day of the week. If you practice handwriting on Tuesday and Thursday from 8 pm to 9 pm, create a schedule that ticks only those two days and add the apps you keep opening. It starts on its own, so you do not have to remember.

If you scroll a lot, add daily limits

Give your most used apps a daily limit in minutes, from 1 minute to 12 hours. Once it is used up the app stays closed for the day, which leaves the evening free. For an app you cannot quit, set 0 minutes and it stays closed all day.

Hand the unlock code to someone at home

With strict mode on, the only way to loosen rules is the unlock code. In strict mode the code is at least 8 characters and works at most 3 times a day, so a partner or friend can keep it. Strict mode also turns on Anti-Uninstall. If you forget the code you can request removal, which takes effect after 72 hours.

Adults on iPhone

iPhone v0.1.5 has no Quick Focus. Create a schedule that repeats on chosen weekdays, such as Tuesday and Thursday evenings, and select short video and social apps. The system enforces it even if Ripple is closed. It can also block deleting apps, protect against clock changes and use an unlock code. It has no daily limits, 0 minute limits or strict mode.

Kids: coloring, play dough and crafts

Agree on the time first

Tell your child that for this hour you are doing a craft or drawing, games and cartoons on the tablet will not open, and afterwards they get the screen time you normally agree on. Let them pick what to make today.

Set a schedule on the child's device

On the child's Android phone or tablet, create a schedule such as Saturday 2 pm to 4 pm and add games, short video and cartoon apps. Keep the phone app and the app they use to reach you off the list.

When the craft comes from a video

If your child follows an origami or drawing video, play it on your own phone or the TV and keep the short video app on their device locked. That way one tutorial does not turn into an hour of recommendations.

Hold tablet games with a daily limit

If your child gets some tablet time each day, give that game a daily limit such as 30 minutes. Once it is used up, the app stays closed for the rest of the day. Set 0 minutes for a game you want gone completely.

Kids cannot turn it off

On Android, add strict mode and Anti-Uninstall and keep the unlock code yourself. Your child cannot turn off the rules or delete Ripple. Android times rules from device uptime, so changing the clock does not help. You can lock your own scrolling apps with the steps above too, see parents going first.

Kids on iPhone or iPad

iPhone v0.1.5 can block apps by schedule. Create a Saturday afternoon schedule so games and short videos do not open. It can also block deleting apps, protect against clock changes and use an unlock code. It has no daily limits, Quick Focus or strict mode. Ripple has no remote management, so set it up directly on the child's device. On Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo, Huawei and Honor phones, follow the keep-alive guide for auto-start and battery settings.

Setup steps

Decide which apps you need while you work

Leave tutorials, reference photos, handwriting apps and the camera off the list. Add only the apps you keep scrolling, such as short video, games and social.

Install Ripple

On Android, download the latest APK and turn on the accessibility service as prompted. On iPhone, install v0.1.5, which needs iOS 16 or later.

Set it before you sit down

For a one-off session on Android use Quick Focus. For regular practice, create a schedule that repeats on those weekdays. On the child's device add strict mode and Anti-Uninstall. On iPhone create a weekly schedule.

When you are done

Scroll afterwards if you like, and hold everyday use with daily limits if you scroll a lot. For kids, see reward time.

FAQ

I need reference photos and tutorials while drawing. Will they be locked?

Not unless they are on the list. With Quick Focus set to lock only the apps I select, only the apps you add are locked.

The tutorial is inside a short video app. What do I do?

Save that video somewhere else first, or play it on the TV or a computer, then lock the short video app on your phone. Otherwise one video easily leads into the recommendations.

I only draw now and then. Do I need weekly rules?

On Android use Quick Focus, which lifts on time. You can also create a schedule that ticks only today and turn it off or delete it afterwards.

My child wants the tablet as soon as the craft is done. What then?

Give the game a daily limit such as 30 minutes, so it closes for the day once used up. Add strict mode and keep the unlock code yourself.

Can I set it up remotely from my own phone?

Not at the moment. Ripple settings stay on the phone they are set on. There is no remote management, so set it up directly on your child's device.

Does it work on iPhone?

iPhone v0.1.5 can block selected apps by schedule, block deleting apps, protect against clock changes and use an unlock code. It has no daily limits, 0 minute limits, Quick Focus or strict mode. Create a schedule that repeats each week.

Does it cost anything? Do I need an account?

It is free, with no ads and no account. See the FAQ for more.
