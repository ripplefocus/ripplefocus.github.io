Ripple vs Flipd and SPACE

Flipd is a study and focus timer. You start a session, it tracks the time and can lock distracting apps while it runs. SPACE tracks how much you use your phone and how often you unlock it, and helps you set goals. Ripple sets rules that stay on all day: schedules, a daily amount per app and an unlock code. I make Ripple, so read this with that in mind. Ripple for Android is v0.1.20 and Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5. Facts about Flipd and SPACE come from Google Play, the Flipd pricing page and the Flipd help center in November 2026. Prices vary by country and change, so check before you pay.

What each one is

Flipd

A focus and study timer for Android and iPhone. You tag sessions such as study or reading and see stats, streaks and leaderboards. It has lofi music, live group study sessions and Full Lock Mode, which locks distracting apps during a session with a whitelist of apps you still need. The free plan has timers up to 40 minutes, one break per session and 30 days of history. Premium is $5.99 a month or $42.99 a year in the US and adds 24 hour timers, unlimited breaks, custom Pomodoro and an editable whitelist.

SPACE

A phone habit app for Android and iPhone. It tracks screen time and unlocks, sorts you into a phone user type, lets you set goals for usage and unlocks, and has tools such as excluding apps, notification blocking and screen dimming, plus reminders and an 8 day course. Google Play says it is free with no ads. SPACE Pro is a one time payment and adds sharing progress with friends and family and comparing yourself with other users.

Ripple

A free app with no ads, no account and no subscription. On Android (8.0 or later) it has schedules, a daily amount per app including 0 minutes, open limits, a break rule, quick focus, blocked websites, a usage calendar and strict mode. On iPhone v0.1.5 it only blocks by schedule, stops apps being deleted, protects the clock and uses an unlock code.

For your own phone

Studying for an exam

If you like timed sessions, stats and studying with others, Flipd is built for that. Ripple has no timer stats or groups. What Ripple adds is rules that stay on when you are not in a session, for example a study schedule from 8 to 12 every weekday and 20 minutes a day of short video.

Knowing your habits

SPACE is good for seeing how often you pick up the phone and setting goals. Ripple shows a usage calendar with time per app per day, and it can cap how many times an app opens, but it does not count unlocks.

Making it hard to cheat

In Ripple, loosening a rule needs a wait, 15 minutes by default, or the unlock code. With strict mode only the code works, at most 3 successful uses a day. You can ask a partner or friend to set the code and keep it.

One hour of focus

Quick focus on Android locks distracting apps right away for a set time, close to Flipd's lock but free and with no 40 minute cap.

For a student's phone

Homework time

A timer app depends on the student starting a session. Ripple can set a homework schedule that starts on its own every school day, and give games and short video apps their own daily amounts.

A game that stays closed

Set one game to 0 minutes and it does not open all day. You can also limit how many times an app opens.

Rules the child cannot undo

Turn on strict mode and keep the code yourself. Anti-Uninstall turns on with strict mode and needs Android 11 or later. Changing the clock does not end a schedule. A forgotten code is lifted 72 hours after a request.

No remote management

Ripple has no parent app. Rules are set on the child's phone, so you need the phone in your hand to change them. Flipd and SPACE are not parental control apps either.

Child on iPhone or iPad

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 can block apps by schedule with a code you keep. It has no daily amounts. For daily limits on iPhone, Screen Time with Family Sharing is free and built in.

How to choose

Pick Flipd

If you want a study timer with stats, music and group sessions, and you lock apps only while a session runs.

Pick SPACE

If you mainly want to understand your phone habits, count unlocks and set goals, with a one time Pro payment.

Pick Ripple

If you want free rules that stay on all day on Android, per app daily amounts and strict mode, for yourself or for a student.

Try it first

Install the latest APK, turn on the Accessibility service when asked, set a 30 minute daily amount on one app and open it to see the block screen. If your phone kills background apps, follow the keep alive guide.

FAQ

Does Ripple have a Pomodoro timer?

No. Ripple has quick focus, which locks distracting apps for a set time, but no Pomodoro cycles or session stats.

Can I use Ripple with Flipd?

Yes. Ripple runs in the background with its own rules, so you can keep a timer app for sessions. Add the timer app to your allowed apps in Ripple schedules.

Is Ripple really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription and no sign up. More in the FAQ.

What does the iPhone version do?

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps by schedule, stops apps being deleted, protects the clock and uses an unlock code. It has no daily amounts.
