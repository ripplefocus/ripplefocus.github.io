Ripple vs YourHour and Regain

YourHour and Regain are two popular Android apps for cutting screen time, both with a free tier and paid Pro plans. YourHour leans on tracking, a floating timer and a pause before you open an app. Regain leans on focus sessions, a Pomodoro timer and study rooms with other people. Ripple is a free Android app with a smaller iPhone version, and it leans on daily amounts per app, a wait before you can loosen a rule, and an unlock code that someone else can keep. I make Ripple, so read this with that in mind. Ripple for Android is v0.1.20 and Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5. Facts about YourHour and Regain come from their Google Play listings and the Regain website in November 2026. Prices differ by country, so check the latest pages before you pay.

What each one is

YourHour

An Android app by Mindefy Labs with over a million downloads on Google Play. It tracks screen time, shows a floating timer on top of apps that turns amber at 80 percent and red at the limit, sets daily and per app limits, blocks Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels, and can show a short breathing or reflection prompt before an app opens. It has a Strict Mode, password protection, reports, and Android TV limits you control from your phone. The Play listing says it contains ads and in-app purchases, with monthly and yearly Pro plans.

Regain

An Android app by EpowerX Labs, with an iPhone version focused on study sessions. It has a focus timer and Pomodoro timer, live focus rooms with leaderboards, an app blocker with Strict Mode that holds until the session ends, Reels and Shorts blocking, a website and adult content blocker, schedules and app limit reminders. Its refund policy lists Regain Pro on Google Play at INR 199 a month or INR 999 a year in India, with offers that can be lower.

Ripple

A free app with no ads, no account and no subscription. On Android (8.0 or later) it has schedules, a daily amount per app including 0 minutes, open limits, a break rule, quick focus, a usage calendar and strict mode. On iPhone v0.1.5 it only blocks by schedule, stops apps being deleted, protects the clock and uses an unlock code.

For your own phone

If you mainly want to see your numbers

YourHour has the richer tracking: the floating timer, weekly and monthly reports and exports in Pro. Ripple shows today's use and a calendar of past days per app, with no exports.

If you only want Reels and Shorts gone

YourHour and Regain can block Reels and Shorts inside Instagram, YouTube and Facebook and leave the rest of the app. Ripple cannot do that. Its partial blocking covers WeChat Channels and Moments, with chats and calls left alone.

If you work in sessions

Regain is built around a timer, Pomodoro breaks and studying alongside others. Ripple has one tap quick focus that locks distracting apps for a set time, and no timer history, sounds or rooms.

If you keep talking yourself out of limits

In Ripple, loosening a rule needs a 15 minute wait (30 minutes for apps like Douyin) or the unlock code. With strict mode only the code works, at most 3 successful uses a day, and you can ask a friend to set the code and keep it.

Ads and payment

Ripple has no ads and nothing to buy. YourHour shows ads in the free version, and some Regain features need Pro.

For a child's phone

Per app limits on a child's Android phone

Ripple can give the game, cartoon and short video apps their own daily amounts, set one game to 0 minutes so it stays closed all day, cap how many times an app opens, and add homework and bedtime schedules.

Rules the child cannot undo

Turn on strict mode and keep the code yourself. Anti-Uninstall turns on with strict mode and needs Android 11 or later. Changing the clock does not end a schedule. A forgotten code is lifted 72 hours after a request.

No remote management

Ripple has no parent app and no remote control, so rules are set on the child's phone. YourHour can limit and lock Android TV apps from your phone, which Ripple cannot do. For a child's phone managed from yours, Google Family Link is the usual choice.

Child on iPhone or iPad

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 can block apps by schedule on the child's device with a code you keep. For daily amounts use Screen Time with Family Sharing.

How to choose

Pick YourHour

If you want detailed tracking, a floating timer and Reels blocking, and you are fine with ads or paying for Pro.

Pick Regain

If you study or work in timed sessions, like focusing with other people, and want Reels and website blocking.

Pick Ripple

If you want free per app daily amounts, open limits, strict mode with a code someone else keeps, or WeChat Channels and Moments blocking, with no ads and no account.

Try it

Install the latest APK, turn on the accessibility service when asked, add one app with a 30 minute daily amount and open it to check the block screen shows. If the phone cleans up background apps, follow the keep alive guide.

FAQ

Can Ripple block only Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts?

No. Ripple limits the whole app. Its partial blocking is for WeChat Channels and Moments.

Does Ripple have a Pomodoro timer or focus rooms?

No. It has one tap quick focus on Android, without timer history or rooms.

Is Ripple really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription and no account. More in the FAQ.

Can I change my child's Ripple rules from my phone?

No. Ripple has no remote management. Rules are set on the child's phone.

What does Ripple for iPhone do?

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps by schedule, can stop apps being deleted, protects against clock changes and uses an unlock code. It has no daily limits.
