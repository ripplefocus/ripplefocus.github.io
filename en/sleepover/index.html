Sleeping over at a friend's place, and the phones stay on until 3 am

A sleepover usually starts with games and snacks. After the lights go off, everyone lies in the dark with a phone, and the next day the kid is exhausted. Adults staying at a friend's place often do the same in the guest bed. I make Ripple, a free app blocker with no account. Its rules are stored on the phone itself, so they keep working at someone else's house. This page shows how I would set it up the day before on Android v0.1.27 and iPhone v0.1.5.

Why a sleepover is different

Someone else's house rules

The other family may have no phone curfew at all, and you will not be there to say goodnight.

The group keeps everyone awake

One kid starts a game or a video and the rest join in. Saying no alone is hard at that age.

It is one night

A rule just for Friday or Saturday night is usually enough. The school week rules can stay as they are.

On a child's phone

A late night schedule for that day

Add a schedule that blocks games, short video and social apps from, say, midnight to 8 am, and pick only the weekday of the sleepover. On iPhone v0.1.5 this runs through Screen Time.

Leave calls and messages open

Pick only the apps that keep kids up, so the phone can still call you if something comes up.

Rules travel with the phone

Rules are stored on the child's phone, so they work at the friend's house the same way they work at home.

Uninstall and clock protection

Ripple can block uninstalling itself and stop changes to the phone clock on Android and iPhone, so moving the time back an hour does not help.

What Ripple cannot do

There is no remote management. You set the rule on the child's phone before they leave and cannot change it from your own phone that night.

On your own phone

A cutoff for the guest bed

Set a schedule on the weekday you are staying over, so short video and games close at the hour you want to sleep.

Quick Focus while you are together

If you would rather talk than scroll, Quick Focus on Android locks only the apps you pick for a set time. Maps, the camera and calls keep working.

Loosening takes a wait

Loosening a rule needs a wait (15 minutes by default, or 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours) or the unlock code. Strict mode leaves only the code.

Setting it up

Install

Android: install the latest APK. iPhone: install v0.1.5 (iOS 16 or later) and allow Screen Time access.

Pick the apps

Choose the games, short video and social apps that keep someone awake.

Add a night schedule for that weekday

Pick the day of the sleepover and the hours. You can switch the schedule off afterwards.

Set the unlock code

Keep it yourself. For your own phone, give it to a friend or partner.

FAQ

Does it need the internet at the friend's house?

No account and no server are involved. The rules are on the phone.

Can I check from my phone whether the rule is working?

No. There is no remote management.

Can iPhone do daily limits for that night?

No. iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps on a schedule, with uninstall protection, clock change protection and an unlock code. Daily limits, open limits and Quick Focus are Android only.

Does it cost anything?

No. Ripple is free, has no ads and no in-app payments.
