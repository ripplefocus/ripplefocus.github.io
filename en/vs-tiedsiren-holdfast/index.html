Ripple vs TiedSiren and Holdfast

TiedSiren comes up when people search for an Android blocker they cannot switch off mid session. Holdfast comes up when people want to keep Instagram or YouTube but lose the Reels and Shorts feed. Ripple is a free app with no account, used by adults on their own phone and by parents on a child's phone. I make Ripple, so read this with that in mind. Ripple for Android is v0.1.26 and Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5. Facts about TiedSiren come from tiedsiren.com and facts about Holdfast come from useholdfast.app, both read in November 2026.

What each one is

TiedSiren

An Android app (8.0 or later) that blocks apps and websites, with blocklists, schedules and a focus timer. Blocking itself is free. Strict Mode, where a session cannot be shortened from the phone and optional locks stop uninstalling and keep Settings closed until the timer ends, needs TiedSiren Pro, a monthly or yearly Google Play subscription with a 7 day trial. A free account is required. Its site says iOS, macOS and Windows are planned.

Holdfast

An Android app that blocks Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok For You and Facebook Reels inside those apps while chats, profiles and search keep working. You can unblock for 5 minutes after a short wait. The free version covers the main feed of one app. Pro covers all four apps and adds a firm mode, locks from an hour to a week, bedtime and schedules. It has no internet permission and no account. There is no iPhone version.

Ripple

A free app with no ads, no account and no subscription. On Android (8.0 or later) it has schedules, a daily amount per app including 0 minutes, open limits, a break rule, quick focus, blocked websites, a usage calendar, limits for only Shorts, Reels, WeChat Channels or Moments inside apps, and strict mode. On iPhone v0.1.5 it only blocks by schedule, stops apps being deleted, protects the clock and uses an unlock code.

For your own phone

A block you cannot cancel in a weak moment

TiedSiren locks a session for a length you pick up front, and that needs Pro. In Ripple strict mode is free: loosening a rule or ending a block early only works with the unlock code, at most 3 successful uses a day, and uninstall protection turns on with it on Android 11 or later. If you give the code to a friend or partner, you cannot undo it yourself. A forgotten code is lifted 72 hours after a request.

Keeping the app but losing the feed

This is what Holdfast is built for, across four apps with Pro. Ripple for Android can limit only Shorts in YouTube, Reels, or WeChat Channels and Moments, so chats keep working. Holdfast lets you unblock for 5 minutes after a wait. In Ripple, loosening takes a 15 minute wait or the code.

Changing the clock

Ripple counts time since the phone booted, so moving the clock forward does not end a block early. I did not see clock changes mentioned on the TiedSiren or Holdfast pages, so check them yourself.

Using an iPhone

TiedSiren and Holdfast are Android only. Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 only blocks apps by schedule with an unlock code, blocks deleting apps and protects the clock. Screen Time in iOS settings is free and already has daily app limits.

For a child's phone

Short video on a child's Android phone

Holdfast is aimed at adults managing their own habit, and its block screen offers a 5 minute unblock. In Ripple a parent can set short video apps to 30 minutes on school days, or limit only Shorts and Channels, turn on strict mode and keep the code. The child cannot loosen it without the code.

Blocking one game completely

Set the game to 0 minutes in Ripple and it will not open all day while other apps keep their amounts. TiedSiren can block it during a session or schedule, and the locked session needs Pro.

Account and privacy

TiedSiren needs a free account. Holdfast and Ripple need none. Holdfast has no internet permission at all. Ripple counts each device once a day with a random number, the platform and the version, and keeps the rules on the phone.

What none of them do

None of the three has a parent app, reports or location. Rules are set on the child's phone in person. If you need to manage from your own phone, look at Google Family Link, which is free, or a paid parental control.

Weak spots of Ripple

Accessibility and battery

Ripple uses the accessibility service like the other two. On Xiaomi, OPPO and similar phones allow autostart and turn off battery saving, or the system may stop it.

Safe mode and reset

Safe mode or a factory reset still gets around Ripple, like any app you install yourself.

Not on Google Play

Ripple is installed from an APK on GitHub or this site. TiedSiren and Holdfast are on Google Play.

Floating windows on OPPO

OPPO floating windows are covered and muted, and you close the window yourself.

How to choose

Choose TiedSiren

You want timed sessions on Android that cannot be shortened, with Settings locked too, and you are fine with an account and a Pro subscription.

Choose Holdfast

You mainly lose time in Reels, Shorts or TikTok For You, want the rest of those apps to work, and like a short wait before a 5 minute unblock.

Choose Ripple

You want strict mode, daily amounts and schedules for free with no account, and an unlock code someone else can keep, for yourself or a child's Android phone.

Try it first

Install the latest APK, turn on the accessibility service when asked, give one app 30 minutes a day and open it to see the block screen before you turn on strict mode.

FAQ

Is strict mode free in Ripple?

Yes. Ripple has no ads, no subscription and no account. More in the FAQ.

Can Ripple block only Reels or Shorts?

On Android it can limit only Shorts, Reels, WeChat Channels or Moments inside apps, and chats keep working. It does not cover TikTok For You or Facebook Reels as separate feeds.

Does Ripple lock the Settings app like TiedSiren?

No. Ripple turns on uninstall protection with strict mode on Android 11 or later, and loosening rules needs the code. It does not keep the whole Settings app closed.

What does the iPhone version do?

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps by schedule, blocks deleting apps, protects the clock and uses an unlock code. It has no daily amounts and no strict mode.
