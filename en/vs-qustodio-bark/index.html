Ripple vs Qustodio and Bark

Parents who search for a parental control app often land on Qustodio or Bark. Both are paid services where a parent app shows what happens on the child's device. Ripple is a free app that sets limits on the phone itself. This page says what Qustodio and Bark do that Ripple does not, and when Ripple is enough, first for parents and then for adults who want limits on their own phone. Ripple for Android is v0.1.20 and Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5. Competitor details and prices come from their US websites and store pages in November 2026, so check their latest notes.

Parents setting up a child's phone

What Qustodio does well

A parent app and a web dashboard show the child's activity from anywhere. It covers Windows, Mac, Android, iPhone and Kindle. The free plan protects one device with basic features such as web filtering, safe search and time limits. Premium Basic is $59.95 a year for up to 5 devices and adds app blocking, location and more. Premium Complete is $104.95 a year for unlimited devices and adds calls and messages monitoring, YouTube monitoring and phone support. Android phones also get a panic button.

What Bark does well

Bark Premium, $99 a year, scans the child's texts, email, YouTube and 30+ apps and platforms and alerts the parent about signs of bullying, self-harm, predators and similar risks. Bark Jr, $49 a year, has screen time schedules, app and website blocking and location check-ins. One subscription covers every child and device in the family. Bark also sells a router device for the home Wi-Fi and its own phone for kids.

First: Ripple has no remote management

Ripple keeps its settings and records on the phone it runs on. You set rules on the child's phone in your hand, and you cannot see the child's screen time or location from your own phone. There is no web content filter and Ripple does not read messages.

What Ripple for Android does on a child's phone

Schedules, for example games and short video apps closed from 7 pm to 9 pm. Daily limits, and a 0 minute limit that keeps one game closed all day. A usage calendar to see how long each app was used on each day. It can close only WeChat Channels and Moments, or YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, while chats keep working. Turn on strict mode and keep the unlock code yourself, and Anti-Uninstall turns on with it. Changing the clock does not end a block. It is free, with no account.

The child uses an iPhone or iPad

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 only blocks by schedule, blocks deleting apps, protects the clock and uses an unlock code. It has no daily limits or usage stats. For more on iPhone, Screen Time with Family Sharing, Qustodio or Bark cover more.

Privacy

To show reports on the parent's phone, Qustodio and Bark send the child's activity to their servers, and Bark's alerts come from scanning content. Ripple uploads no usage content. It only sends an anonymous count once a day with the platform, version, days since install, new install or update, app language and a random install ID made on the phone.

Adults limiting their own phone

Qustodio and Bark are made for families

Both are set up as a parent account watching a child's device. An adult could hold the parent account for their own phone, but the reports, alerts and location features are built for parents, and Bark's message scanning is aimed at kids' safety.

What Ripple for Android does for adults

Schedules, daily limits, Quick Focus, an open limit per app and a pause before opening. In strict mode, rules can only be loosened with an unlock code of at least 8 characters, used successfully at most 3 times a day, which you can give to a partner or friend. A forgotten code can be requested and is lifted after 72 hours.

What Ripple cannot do for adults

There is no desktop app or browser extension, so it does not block websites on a computer. Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 only blocks by schedule. The APK comes from the website and Ripple is not on Google Play or the App Store.

How to choose

You want to see the child's activity and location from your own phone

Choose Qustodio or Bark, or the free Google Family Link on Android and Family Sharing on iPhone. Ripple cannot do this.

You worry about what the child sees and receives in messages and social apps

Choose Bark Premium. Its main feature is alerts from scanning messages and social apps.

The family has Windows, Mac or Kindle devices too

Qustodio covers more device types.

You can set up the child's Android phone in person and want limits that are free and hard to remove

Install Ripple for Android, set schedules or daily limits, turn on strict mode and keep the unlock code. Some parents use Family Link to look at reports and Ripple for the limits. See parental control.

You want limits on your own phone

Use Ripple for Android and give the strict mode code to someone you trust.

FAQ

Is Ripple a free Qustodio or Bark alternative?

For limits set on the child's Android phone, yes, and it is free. For remote reports, location, web filtering or message alerts, no. Ripple has none of those.

Can Ripple show my child's screen time on my phone?

No. Ripple has no remote management. Check the usage calendar on the child's phone.

Does Ripple read my child's messages?

No. It only checks which app or WeChat page is open so it can block it. No usage content is uploaded.

Can I use Ripple together with Family Link or Qustodio?

Yes, they can be on the same phone. Each asks for its own permissions, so grant them as each one explains.

Does it cost anything?

Ripple is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. More in the FAQ.
