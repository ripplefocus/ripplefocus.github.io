Revenge bedtime scrolling, and kids on the phone after you fall asleep

After a day of work and housework, the hour after 10 p.m. can feel like the only time that is yours. You pick up the phone to relax, look up and it is 1 a.m., and the next morning is harder. Many parents of young kids know this well. Kids have their own version. Some wait until the adults are asleep, take the phone from the charger and play games or watch videos under the covers. Ripple for Android is v0.1.20 and Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5. The first part is for adults and the second part is for parents.

Adults: the hour that feels like yours

Keep the evening, set the end

Late night scrolling is often about wanting some time of your own, so blocking everything from 9 p.m. rarely lasts. Choose the end time instead. A schedule from 23:30 to 7:00 with short video, short dramas, games and novel apps on the list leaves the evening open and closes them when you want to be asleep.

Give the main app an amount

If one app takes most of the night, give it a daily limit such as 45 minutes. You can use it at lunch or at 10 p.m., and once the time is used it stays closed for the day. A 0 minute limit keeps an app closed all day.

Fewer quick checks in bed

For the app you keep reopening after you put the phone down, add a daily open limit and a pause before opening. Each open waits 5 to 30 seconds and shows how long you have used the app today. See open limits.

If you tend to undo it at midnight

Without strict mode, loosening a rule needs a wait of 15 minutes, or the unlock code. With strict mode on, only the unlock code works, with at most 3 successful uses a day, so give it to your partner. Strict mode also turns on Anti-Uninstall. A forgotten code can be requested and is lifted after 72 hours.

Nights with no way out

Turn on Complete Lock for the sleep schedule and only system essentials such as phone, messages and alarms open until it ends. The unlock code cannot lift it. Calls and the morning alarm still work.

Look back at the week

The usage calendar on Android shows each day's total and each app's time, opens and blocks. Check the days you went to bed late and see which app took the time.

On iPhone

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps by schedule, so a 23:30 to 7:00 schedule works there too. It can also block deleting apps, protect against clock changes and use an unlock code. It has no daily limits, open limits, strict mode or Complete Lock.

Parents: the phone after lights out

Rules that stay on the phone

Taking the phone away at night works until the child finds where it is charging. Ripple rules stay on the child's phone wherever it is, so a game opened under the covers at 1 a.m. still shows the block screen.

A school night schedule

Add a schedule such as 21:30 to 6:30 on school nights with games, short video, live streaming and novel apps on the list. Phone calls, messages and alarms always work.

Complete Lock for the night

With Complete Lock on, that schedule closes every app except system essentials such as phone, messages and alarms, so the browser and other apps cannot be used for videos either. It ends in the morning when the schedule ends.

They cannot switch it off or change the clock

Turn on strict mode and keep the unlock code yourself. Anti-Uninstall turns on with it. Time is counted from device uptime, so moving the clock forward does not end the night schedule. See clock changes.

If they take your phone instead

Rules on your phone apply to whoever is holding it. A night schedule on your own phone also stops a child who takes it from your bedside. See kids borrowing your phone.

Talk it over with the calendar

The next day, open the usage calendar on the child's phone. A block count on the day after a late night shows whether games were tried, which is an easier way to start the conversation than a guess.

Child on iPhone or iPad

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 can block games and video apps on a night schedule, block deleting apps, protect the clock and use an unlock code. On iPhone you can also use Downtime in Screen Time with Family Sharing.

Setup steps

Install or update

On Android download the latest APK and turn on the accessibility service when asked, or update to v0.1.20 in Settings › About › Check for updates. On iPhone install v0.1.5, which needs iOS 16 or later.

Adults: pick the end time

Add a sleep schedule that starts when you want to be asleep, give your main app a daily limit, and give the unlock code to your partner if you turn on strict mode.

Parents: set the child's night

Add a school night schedule, turn on Complete Lock for it if the child watches videos in other apps, then turn on strict mode and Anti-Uninstall and keep the code.

After a week

Check the usage calendar and move the times if they turned out too strict or too loose.

FAQ

What is revenge bedtime procrastination?

It means staying up late on the phone because the night feels like the only free time of the day, even when you know you need sleep. A night schedule with a fixed end and an unlock code someone else keeps helps with the ending.

Will alarms and calls still work at night?

Yes. Phone, messages and alarms always open, including during Complete Lock.

My child takes the phone after we fall asleep. What can I set?

On the child's Android phone add a night schedule with Complete Lock, turn on strict mode and Anti-Uninstall, and keep the code yourself. If they use your phone, add a night schedule on yours too.

Does it work on iPhone?

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps by schedule and can block deleting apps. Daily limits, strict mode and Complete Lock are on Android only for now.

Does it cost anything?

Ripple is free, with no ads and no account. More in the FAQ.
