Can't stop checking likes and comments after posting

You post a photo, a note or a short video, and for the next hour or two you keep opening the app to see how many likes it has and whether anyone commented. Each check is only a quick look, but over an evening it adds up to dozens of opens, and you end up scrolling other things along the way. Kids do the same after posting a video: they keep refreshing the views and comments and pick up the phone every few minutes during homework. Ripple helps you decide how many times those apps can open each day, how long they can be used and when they stay closed. The first part is for adults setting this up for themselves and the second part is for parents agreeing on rules with their kids. Android is on v0.1.19 and iPhone on v0.1.5, with different features, so they are described separately.

Adults: post it, put the phone down, check later

Cap opens per day

The most direct fix is a daily open limit on the apps you post to, for example 8 opens a day. Once they are used up the app stays closed for the day. See the open limit page.

Pause before opening

With the pause before opening on, the screen first shows how many times you opened the app today and how long you used it. Seeing that number is often enough to close it.

Give those apps a daily limit

On Android you can also set a daily limit in minutes, for example 30 minutes a day. The scrolling you do while checking likes counts toward it.

WeChat Moments and Channels

WeChat is needed for chat, so blocking it entirely is rarely practical. Since Android v0.1.9 Moments and Channels can get a few minutes first, then you write a reason to get 5 more minutes, up to 3 times a day. See the Moments allowance page.

Lock it right after posting

The urge to refresh is strongest right after you post. Tap Quick Focus, pick a length, and listed apps close at once. Read the comments after it ends.

If you use an iPhone

iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps by schedule, for example after 22:00 and during work hours. It can also block deleting apps, protect against clock changes and use an unlock code. It has no open limits, pause before opening, daily limits, Quick Focus or strict mode.

Parents: when your child keeps checking views on a video

Talk about it first

It is normal for kids to care about likes. Talk with your child first and agree on how often to check after posting and how long to spend on those apps each day, then put that into Ripple.

Limit opens and time

On Android give TikTok, Kuaishou or Xiaohongshu a daily open limit and a daily limit, for example 30 minutes on school days. Once either runs out the app stays closed for the day, so you do not have to keep watching.

Closed during homework and sleep

Add two schedules, for example 18:30 to 21:00 for homework and 22:00 to 6:30 for sleep, and those apps close then. See the homework page.

The child cannot change it

On Android turn on strict mode. Rules can then only be loosened with the unlock code, which in strict mode is at least 8 characters, works at most 3 times a day and stays with the parent. Strict mode turns on Anti-Uninstall too, so the child cannot remove Ripple. Ripple on Android counts time since boot, so changing the system clock does not add time.

Keep alive settings on Chinese brand phones

Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo, Huawei and Honor phones may stop Ripple in the background. After installing, follow the keep alive guide to set autostart and battery optimization.

If the child uses an iPhone or iPad

iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps by schedule, so those apps do not open during homework and sleep time. It can also block deleting apps, protect against clock changes and use an unlock code. It has no open limits or daily limits. See the parental control page.

Setup steps

Install Ripple

On Android download the latest APK and turn on the accessibility service as guided. On iPhone install v0.1.5, which needs iOS 16 or later.

Pick the apps you post to

Put Xiaohongshu, TikTok, Kuaishou, Weibo or whatever apps you post on and keep checking on the list. Leave chat, maps and payment apps off.

Set open limits, daily limits and schedules

On Android give those apps a daily open limit, the pause before opening and a daily limit, then add bedtime and homework schedules. On iPhone add schedules for bedtime, homework or work hours.

Hand over the unlock code

On Android turn on strict mode and Anti-Uninstall. Give the unlock code to a family member or friend, and keep the code for a child's phone yourself.

FAQ

I keep checking likes after I post. How do I stop?

In Ripple on Android give that app a daily open limit, turn on the pause before opening and add a daily limit. Right after posting you can tap Quick Focus to lock it for a while.

My child keeps refreshing the views on a video. What can I do?

Agree with your child on how often to check, then give those apps an open limit and a daily limit and add homework and sleep schedules. On Android turn on strict mode and Anti-Uninstall and keep the unlock code.

Can WeChat Moments be limited too?

Since Android v0.1.9 Moments and Channels can get a few minutes first, then you write a reason for 5 more minutes, up to 3 times a day. In strict mode the unlock code is used instead, and the time counts toward WeChat's daily limit. Chat is not affected.

Does it work on iPhone?

iPhone v0.1.5 blocks selected apps by schedule, can block deleting apps, protects against clock changes and has an unlock code. It has no open limits, pause before opening or daily limits.

Can a child get more time by changing the clock?

On Android Ripple counts time since boot, so changing the system clock does not add time. The iPhone version protects against clock changes.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

It is free with no ads and no account. See the FAQ for more.
