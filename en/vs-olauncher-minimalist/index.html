Ripple vs Olauncher and minimalist phone

Olauncher and minimalist phone replace your Android home screen with a plain list of app names. The idea is that with no icons, badges or colors you open fewer apps out of habit. Ripple works differently. It keeps your normal home screen and sets rules on the apps themselves: daily amounts, open limits, schedules and a wait before you can loosen a rule. I make Ripple, so read this with that in mind. Ripple for Android is v0.1.20 and Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5. Facts about Olauncher and minimalist phone come from their Google Play listings in November 2026. Plans and prices can change, so check the latest listing before you pay.

What each one is

Olauncher

A free and open source Android launcher (GPLv3) by Tanuj Mishra, with over a million downloads on Google Play. The home screen shows a few app names as text, with no icons and no ads. You can hide apps, rename them, change text size, use swipe gestures and get a new wallpaper each day. The listing says it collects no data. It does not block apps or set time limits. A paid sibling app, Pro Launcher, adds things like a weather widget.

minimalist phone

An Android launcher by Hachi Media with over five million downloads on Google Play. Besides the text only home screen it has an app blocker, blocking schedules, blocking short videos in YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and Snapchat, a website blocker, in-app time reminders, a notification filter and grayscale for chosen apps. The listing says it has monthly, yearly and one time plans, each with a free trial.

Ripple

A free app with no ads, no account and no subscription. It is an app blocker and leaves your launcher as it is. On Android (8.0 or later) it has schedules, a daily amount per app including 0 minutes, open limits, a break rule, quick focus, blocked websites, a usage calendar and strict mode. On iPhone v0.1.5 it only blocks by schedule, stops apps being deleted, protects the clock and uses an unlock code.

For your own phone

If icons and badges pull you in

A text only home screen is the simplest fix and Olauncher does it for free. Ripple does not change how your home screen looks.

If you open the app anyway

A launcher makes an app harder to see. You can still search for it or open it from a notification. Ripple counts the time inside the app and shows a block screen when the daily amount or open limit is used up, wherever you opened it from.

If you switch launchers back on a bad night

Going back to the stock launcher takes a few taps in Settings. In Ripple, loosening a rule needs a wait, 15 minutes by default, or the unlock code. With strict mode only the code works, at most 3 successful uses a day, and a friend can set the code and keep it.

Short videos

minimalist phone can block short videos inside YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and Snapchat. Ripple limits whole apps. Its partial blocking covers WeChat Channels and Moments, with chats and calls left alone.

Using both

Ripple works with any launcher, so you can keep Olauncher for a quiet home screen and use Ripple for the limits.

For a child's phone

A launcher on a child's phone

A child can change the default launcher back in Settings, so a launcher alone is easy to undo. It also does not limit how long a game runs.

Per app limits

Ripple can give game, cartoon and short video apps their own daily amounts, set one game to 0 minutes so it stays closed all day, cap how many times an app opens, and add homework and bedtime schedules.

Rules the child cannot undo

Turn on strict mode and keep the code yourself. Anti-Uninstall turns on with strict mode and needs Android 11 or later. Changing the clock does not end a schedule. A forgotten code is lifted 72 hours after a request.

No remote management

Ripple has no parent app and no remote control, so rules are set on the child's phone. Google Family Link is the usual choice if you want to manage from your own phone.

Child on iPhone or iPad

Third party launchers do not exist on iPhone. Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 can block apps by schedule on the child's device with a code you keep. For daily amounts use Screen Time with Family Sharing.

How to choose

Pick Olauncher

If what you want is a free, open source, plain home screen and you trust yourself to stop once the icons are gone.

Pick minimalist phone

If you want the plain home screen and blocking in one app, including short video blocking, and you are fine paying after the trial.

Pick Ripple

If you want free per app daily amounts, open limits, website blocking and strict mode with a code someone else keeps, on any launcher, with no account.

Try it

Install the latest APK, turn on the accessibility service when asked, give one app a 30 minute daily amount and open it to check the block screen shows. If the phone cleans up background apps, follow the keep alive guide.

FAQ

Is Ripple a launcher?

No. Ripple keeps your current home screen. You can use it together with Olauncher or any other launcher.

Can Ripple hide apps from the home screen?

No. It limits and blocks apps. Hiding apps is what a launcher does.

Can Ripple block only YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels?

No. Ripple limits the whole app. Its partial blocking is for WeChat Channels and Moments.

Is Ripple really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription and no account. More in the FAQ.

What does Ripple for iPhone do?

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps by schedule, can stop apps being deleted, protects against clock changes and uses an unlock code. It has no daily limits.
