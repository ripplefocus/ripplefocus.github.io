When an app is blocked, go listen to rain or do slow breathing

A schedule starts, the daily time runs out, or Wait a moment is still counting. A lot of people spend that time staring at the block screen, looking for a way back in. Kids often spend it asking a parent for the unlock code. I make Ripple, a free app blocker with no account. Since Android v0.1.24, those block screens offer "Put the phone down and relax for a bit", so you can open sleep sounds or slow breathing. The relax page has no way back to the blocked app. Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5 and does not have this.

What was added in v0.1.28

Which screens show it

The block screens for schedules, Quick Focus, Complete Lock, daily limit reached, session cap, opens used up, and Wait a moment now show "Put the phone down and relax for a bit", with Sleep sounds and Breathe.

No way back to the blocked app

The relax page has no button that reopens the app you were just blocked from.

Wait a moment works a little differently

If you tap relax on the Wait a moment screen, this open is given up. The app is not let through, and the open is not counted. Opening it again starts the wait from the beginning.

Where it does not appear

Screens for apps set to Open After Limit, the screen shown when short video time runs out, and blocked website screens do not show this entry. A night schedule that already has the sleep entry turned on keeps the "Can't sleep? Play a sleep sound" card.

A timer for slow breathing

On the breathing page you can set it to stop after 3, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes. After you finish or tap End, the background sound fades out and stops.

On your own phone

When the limit hits

If you usually try to add more time as soon as a daily limit or a schedule starts, open Breathe or Sleep sounds on that screen and stay there for a few minutes.

If you use Wait a moment

Tapping relax means you are not opening the app this time. That is useful when you only picked it up out of habit.

Loosening a rule is unchanged

Loosening a rule still needs a wait (15 minutes by default, or 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours) or the unlock code. Strict mode leaves only the code.

iPhone

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps on a schedule through Screen Time, with uninstall protection, protection against clock changes and an unlock code. It has no block-screen relax entry, sleep sounds or slow breathing.

On a child's phone

Something to do after a block

When a game or short video app is blocked on a child's phone, they can listen to rain or follow the breathing circle. They cannot get back into the blocked app from that page.

The code stays with you

Set the rules and the unlock code on the child's phone yourself and keep the code.

What Ripple cannot do

There is no remote management. You set everything on the child's phone and cannot see or change it from your own phone.

Setting it up

Update

Install Android v0.1.24 or later over your current version. Your rules stay.

Keep a rule that can trigger a block

A schedule, a daily limit, Quick Focus, Complete Lock, a session cap, an opens cap, or Wait a moment is enough.

Try it once

Open a blocked app so the block screen appears, then tap Sleep sounds or Breathe.

Optional breathing timer

If you open Breathe, you can set it to stop after 3, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes.

FAQ

Has this been tested on a real phone?

The relax card has been checked on a real phone only on the Wait a moment screen. The schedule, Quick Focus, Complete Lock, daily limit, session cap and opens used up screens have not been checked on a real phone yet. Long sleep sound playback with the screen off, incoming calls and unplugging headphones have not been tested on a real phone either.

Can my kid use the relax page to get back into the game?

No. The relax page has no way back to the blocked app.

Does the iPhone version have this?

No. iPhone v0.1.5 only blocks on a schedule, with uninstall protection, clock change protection and the unlock code.

Does it cost anything?

No. Ripple is free, has no ads and no in-app payments.
