Ripple vs BlockSite and Lock Me Out

BlockSite and Lock Me Out come up often when people look for an app or site blocker. BlockSite started as a browser extension and now has Android and iPhone apps. Lock Me Out is an Android app that has been around since 2014. Ripple is a free app blocker for Android and iPhone. This page says what the two do that Ripple does not, and when Ripple is enough, for adults limiting their own phone and for parents. Ripple for Android is v0.1.19 and Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5. Details about BlockSite and Lock Me Out come from their websites, help pages and store pages in November 2026, so check their latest notes.

What BlockSite and Lock Me Out do well

BlockSite

There is a browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Firefox, plus Android and iPhone apps. It blocks sites and apps from a list, by keyword or by category such as adult or social sites. It has schedules, daily usage limits, a Focus Mode with timed breaks, a custom block page and password protection. Block lists sync between desktop browsers and between Android phones. Many of these are Premium features. Its site sells Premium as a $49.90 lifetime plan, and the iPhone app also offers monthly and yearly subscriptions.

Lock Me Out

An Android app on Google Play. It has three modes: block chosen apps, allow only chosen apps, or allow only the lock screen. Lockouts can run on a schedule or start from usage, such as time in apps, total screen time, number of app opens or number of unlocks. It can also block or allow websites, lock only at chosen places, hide notifications from blocked apps, turn on Do Not Disturb, add regular breaks and give temporary emergency access. Premium is a monthly, yearly or one time payment and adds uninstall and tampering protection, unlimited lockouts and location rules.

What Ripple does not have

No browser extension or desktop app, so websites on a computer are not covered. No keyword or category blocking: on Android you add websites one domain at a time, up to 100. No location rules, Do Not Disturb control or hiding of notifications. No sync between devices and no remote management. Ripple is not on Google Play or the App Store, and the APK comes from the website.

Adults limiting their own phone

Strict mode and Anti-Uninstall are free

In Lock Me Out the uninstall and tampering protection is part of Premium, and in BlockSite password protection is a Premium feature. In Ripple for Android, strict mode means rules can only be loosened with an unlock code, used successfully at most 3 times a day, which you can give to a partner or friend. Anti-Uninstall turns on with it. A forgotten code can be requested and is lifted after 72 hours.

Limits by time and by opens

Schedules, daily limits, 0 minute limits, an open limit per app with a pause before opening, Quick Focus and Complete Lock. Lock Me Out has similar usage rules and adds lockouts based on the number of unlocks.

Only part of an app

Ripple can close only WeChat Channels and Moments while chats keep working, and the same for YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. If short video inside a chat app is your problem, this matters.

Clock changes and a usage calendar

Time is counted from device uptime, so changing the clock does not end a block. The usage calendar keeps 365 days on the phone, with each app's time, opens and blocks per day.

On iPhone

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 only blocks by schedule, blocks deleting apps, protects the clock and uses an unlock code. BlockSite has more on iPhone, including website blocking. Lock Me Out has no iPhone app.

Parents setting up a child's phone

Set it on the child's Android phone

Ripple is free with no account. Add homework and sleep schedules, give games daily limits or 0 minutes, turn on strict mode and keep the unlock code. Anti-Uninstall turns on with it, and clock changes do nothing. See parental control.

Adult sites in the browser

BlockSite's category blocking covers adult sites in one step. Ripple does not filter content by category. You can add specific sites to Blocked websites on Android, or give the browser a daily limit.

Lock screen only at night

Lock Me Out's lock screen only mode is a strict option for nights. Ripple has Complete Lock on a schedule, which leaves only system essentials such as phone, messages and alarms until the schedule ends.

Privacy

BlockSite uses an account so Premium and sync work across devices. Ripple needs no account and uploads no usage content. It only sends an anonymous count once a day with the platform, version, days since install, new install or update, app language and a random install ID made on the phone.

How to choose

You also need to block sites in a computer browser

Choose BlockSite. Ripple has no desktop version.

You want to block whole categories of sites or keywords

Choose BlockSite Premium.

You want rules that turn on at a place, or Do Not Disturb during lockouts

Choose Lock Me Out Premium.

You want an app from Google Play

Choose Lock Me Out or BlockSite. Ripple is installed from its website.

You want strict limits with uninstall protection on Android without paying

Install Ripple for Android, set schedules or daily limits, and turn on strict mode with the code kept by someone else.

Most of your time goes to WeChat Channels, Douyin or Shorts

Ripple can close only those parts and keep chats working.

FAQ

Is Ripple a free Lock Me Out alternative?

For schedules, daily limits, open limits and uninstall protection on Android, yes, and it is free. For location rules, unlock count lockouts or Do Not Disturb control, no.

Is Ripple a BlockSite alternative?

On an Android phone it covers app limits and blocking listed websites. It has no browser extension, no keyword or category blocking and no sync.

Can I use Ripple with BlockSite or Lock Me Out?

Yes. For example BlockSite on the computer and Ripple on the phone. Two blockers on one phone each ask for the accessibility service, so give them different apps to keep their rules from overlapping.

Does it cost anything?

Ripple is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. More in the FAQ.
