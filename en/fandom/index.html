Can't stop scrolling fan pages and idol videos

When you are a fan of someone, the phone is hard to put down. You keep checking the fan page for new posts, fancams and live clips play one after another, and waiting for an announcement can keep you up past midnight. Adult fans often start after work and look up at 1 or 2 am, then feel it the next day. Kids get pulled in even more: watching under the covers at night, thinking about voting while doing homework. Ripple does not care who you follow. It only helps you decide how much time those apps get each day and when they stay closed. The first part is for adults setting this up for themselves and the second part is for parents agreeing on rules with their kids. Android is on v0.1.17 and iPhone on v0.1.5, with different features, so they are described separately.

Adults: keep being a fan without losing sleep and work time

Give fan apps a daily limit

On Android give Weibo, TikTok, Bilibili or whichever apps you use for idol content a daily limit in minutes, for example 40 minutes a day. Once it is used up they stay closed for the day, and new posts can wait until tomorrow.

Check the fan page less often

If you keep opening the fan page for a quick look, cap how many times that app can open per day and turn on the pause before opening. Before it opens the screen shows how many times you opened it today and how long you used it. See the open limit page.

Close it at night

Late nights are where most of the time goes. Add a schedule such as 23:30 to 7:00, and listed apps close at that time. See the bedtime page.

Leave it alone during work

If you keep switching over at work, add a weekday schedule from 9:00 to 18:00, or tap Quick Focus before a meeting and pick a length.

If you tend to undo your rules

Turn on strict mode on Android. Rules can then only be loosened with the unlock code. In strict mode the code is at least 8 characters and works at most 3 times a day. Give it to a family member or friend. Strict mode turns on Anti-Uninstall too, and if you forget the code you can request removal, which takes effect after 72 hours.

If you use an iPhone

iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps by schedule, so you can add one schedule for bedtime and one for work hours, and fan apps will not open then. It can also block deleting apps, protect against clock changes and use an unlock code. It has no daily limits, open limits, pause before opening, Quick Focus or strict mode.

Parents: when your child follows an idol, agree on the time together

Talk first, then set it up

Liking an idol is fine, and taking the phone away usually ends in a fight. Agree together on how long they can spend on idol content each day and what time it stops, then put that into Ripple. Kids accept rules they helped make more easily.

How long each day

Give Weibo, TikTok and Bilibili a daily limit, for example 30 minutes on school days and a bit more on weekends. Once it is used up they stay closed for the day, so you do not have to chase them every night.

No fan apps during homework and sleep

Add two schedules, for example 18:30 to 21:00 for homework and 22:00 to 6:30 for sleep, and those apps close then. Staying up to vote or watch a midnight live stream happens far less. See the homework page.

The child cannot change it

On Android with strict mode, rules can only be loosened with the unlock code, which the parent keeps. With Anti-Uninstall on, the child cannot remove Ripple. Ripple on Android counts time since boot, so changing the system clock does not add time. Listed apps are also blocked in split screen, floating windows and PiP. On OPPO phones a floating window can only be covered and muted, and it has to be closed by hand.

Keep alive settings on Chinese brand phones

Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo, Huawei and Honor phones may stop Ripple in the background. After installing, follow the keep alive guide to set autostart and battery optimization.

If the child uses an iPhone or iPad

iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps by schedule, so fan apps do not open during homework and sleep time. It can also block deleting apps, protect against clock changes and use an unlock code. It has no daily limits or open limits. See the parental control page.

Setup steps

Install Ripple

On Android download the latest APK and turn on the accessibility service as guided. On iPhone install v0.1.5, which needs iOS 16 or later.

Pick the fan apps

Put Weibo, TikTok, Bilibili, Xiaohongshu or whatever apps carry idol content on the list. Leave chat, maps and payment apps off.

Set daily limits, open limits and schedules

On Android give those apps a daily limit, a daily open limit and the pause before opening, then add a bedtime schedule. On iPhone add schedules for bedtime, homework or work hours.

Hand over the unlock code

On Android turn on strict mode and Anti-Uninstall. Give the unlock code to a family member or friend, and keep the code for a child's phone yourself.

FAQ

I can't stop scrolling fan pages. How do I control it?

In Ripple on Android give the fan apps a daily limit and a daily open limit, and turn on the pause before opening. Add a bedtime schedule so they close at night.

My child stays up late on the phone following an idol. What can I do?

Agree with your child on daily time and a cutoff hour, give the fan apps a daily limit and add homework and sleep schedules. On Android turn on strict mode and Anti-Uninstall and keep the unlock code.

Will chat and study apps be locked too?

Not if they are off the list. Only Complete Lock in a schedule leaves just the system essential apps.

Does it work on iPhone?

iPhone v0.1.5 blocks selected apps by schedule, can block deleting apps, protects against clock changes and has an unlock code. It has no daily limits, open limits or pause before opening.

Can a child get more time by changing the clock?

On Android Ripple counts time since boot, so changing the system clock does not add time. The iPhone version protects against clock changes.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

It is free with no ads and no account. See the FAQ for more.
