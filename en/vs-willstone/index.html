Ripple vs WillStone (自律石头)

WillStone (自律石头) trades good habits such as reading, vocabulary practice and steps for entertainment time, is listed on the App Store and major Android stores, and puts some features behind a membership. Ripple is fully free with no account and focuses on limits you cannot switch off on impulse: Android v0.1.31 has strict mode, an unlock code, Anti-Uninstall and clock change resistance, but no habit exchange and no app store listing.

What WillStone does well

The exchange gives a reason to do useful things, since reading, vocabulary and exercise all earn time. The stone character and reminders are fun, and the stats pages and widgets are detailed. It is on the main app stores with more than ten thousand iOS ratings, so it is easy to install.

What Ripple does well

Ripple is built so impulses do not win. Tightening applies at once, and loosening waits 15 minutes by default or needs an unlock code given to someone else. Strict mode, Anti-Uninstall and clock change resistance are free on Android, and blocked apps stay blocked in split screen or floating windows. In WeChat it can close only Channels and Moments. Parents can set up a child's phone that the child cannot switch off.

Where Ripple falls short

It is not on app stores, so you download the APK from the website and turn on Accessibility and Device Admin. The iPhone version v0.1.5 only has time windows, blocking deletion, clock change resistance and the unlock code. There is no habit exchange and no remote management.

FAQ

Should I pick WillStone or Ripple?

If you want to earn screen time with good habits and like playful feedback, WillStone fits. If you keep turning limits off or uninstalling the blocker, Ripple fits better.

Is Ripple free? Is WillStone?

Ripple is fully free with no ads and no membership. WillStone's basic limits are free and some features need a membership. Public reviews mention a lifetime membership of 58 yuan; check the in app price.

Does Ripple let me earn time with good habits like WillStone?

No. Ripple has daily limits, time windows, open limits, a pause before opening, 5 more minutes after writing a reason (WeChat Channels and Moments) and quick focus. It has no step or study time exchange.

Can Ripple stop uninstalling?

Ripple for Android v0.1.31 can. With Anti-Uninstall on, uninstalling waits for a countdown or needs an unlock code someone else keeps. In strict mode loosening needs the code, with at most 3 successful uses a day. Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 can block deleting apps.

Which one on iPhone?

WillStone on iPhone has more features, including the exchange and stats, plus iPad and Mac versions. Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 only blocks by time window, prevents deleting apps, resists clock changes and uses an unlock code. It has no daily limits and is not on the App Store.
