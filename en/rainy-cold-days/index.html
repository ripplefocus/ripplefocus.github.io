Less scrolling on rainy or cold days at home

You planned to go out at the weekend, then it rains, or the temperature drops and the wind picks up, so you stay in. You lean back on the sofa or never get out of bed, and short videos and short dramas carry you until it is dark outside with nothing else done. Once winter sets in it is already dark when you get home from work, and the phone gets more use than in summer. Kids are the same. When it rains they cannot play outside, in winter it is dark soon after school, and with nothing to do they pick up the tablet for cartoons and games and stay on it all afternoon. Ripple can give these apps a daily allowance that locks them when it runs out, and can make you pause after a long stretch. The weekend page covers weekends and holidays in general. This page is about the days when the weather keeps you indoors. The first part is for adults setting limits for themselves, and the second part is for parents managing a child's tablet or phone. Android is at v0.1.20 and iPhone at v0.1.5. They can do different things, so each is described separately.

Adults: scroll less on a day indoors

Daily limits on short video and short drama

A whole day at home is long, and short drama episodes can fill an afternoon. Give these apps a daily limit in minutes, for example 60 minutes a day. When it runs out they stay closed until the next day. See the short dramas page.

Turn on Take a Break

On the sofa it is easy to scroll for a long time without moving. With Take a Break, for example 30 minutes of use and then a 5 minute break, entertainment apps pause once you reach the limit. Use the break to get a drink or tidy up. See the eye break page.

A short wait before opening, and an open limit

With nothing to do you pick up the phone again and again. Turn on the short wait before each open for short video apps and set how many times a day they can be opened. When the opens run out, the app stays closed for the day. See the open limit page.

One-tap focus when you want to read or learn

A day at home is a good chance to read or study. Start a focus session from the home screen with one tap and pick a length. Short video and games stay closed until it ends. See the one-tap focus page.

Strict mode if boredom makes you switch rules off

After hours at home it is easy to decide that today does not count. With strict mode, rules can only be relaxed with an unlock code. Give a code of at least 8 digits to someone at home. An unlock code can succeed at most 3 times a day. Strict mode also turns on Anti-Uninstall. If the code is lost you can request removal, which takes effect after 72 hours. See the strict mode page.

Parents: no playing outside, so the tablet comes out all afternoon

Plan a few things to do indoors

On days when they cannot go out, kids often reach for the tablet because there is nothing else to do. Have a few indoor options ready, such as puzzles, building blocks, drawing or cooking together, and agree with your child how long the tablet can be used today.

Daily limits on the child's tablet or old phone

On the child's Android tablet or old phone, set daily limits on games and short video. Rainy days count the same, and the apps lock when time runs out. If one game is especially hard to stop, set it to 0 minutes for now. See the mobile games page.

Take a Break and a bedtime schedule

Set Take a Break to 20 minutes of use and a 10 minute break, so your child has to stop now and then. Add a bedtime schedule from 9 pm to 7 am with games, short video and cartoon apps on the list, so a late day indoors still ends on time. Keep calls, WeChat and the apps used to reach parents off the list.

Split screen, floating windows and clock changes

On Android, listed apps are also blocked in split screen, floating windows and picture-in-picture. On OPPO phones a floating window can only be covered and muted, and the window itself has to be closed by hand. Ripple for Android counts time since boot, so changing the system clock does not add time. See the clock change page.

Parents keep the unlock code and turn on Anti-Uninstall; iPhone

Only parents know the unlock code, and with Anti-Uninstall on Android the child cannot remove Ripple. Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 can block apps by schedule, block deleting apps, resist clock changes and use an unlock code, so you can add schedules for weekend afternoons and evenings. Daily limits, 0 minutes, open limits, Take a Break, one-tap focus and strict mode are Android only for now. See the parental control page.

Setup steps (Android v0.1.20)

Install or update to the latest version

On Android, download the latest APK and turn on the accessibility service as guided. If you have an older version, update in Settings › About › Check for updates. On iPhone install v0.1.5, which needs iOS 16 or later.

Adult phone: daily limits and open limits on short video and short drama, and turn on Take a Break

Keep calls, WeChat and maps off the list.

Child's tablet or old phone: agree with your child, then set daily limits or 0 minutes on games and short video, turn on Take a Break and add a bedtime schedule

Keep the apps used to reach parents off the list.

Set an unlock code and turn on Anti-Uninstall on the child's device

Adults give their code to someone at home, and parents keep the code for the child's device. Turn on Anti-Uninstall on Android, and strict mode if you need it to hold. On iPhone, turn on the block on deleting apps.

FAQ

When it rains or gets cold I stay home and scroll all day. What can I set?

On Ripple for Android v0.1.20, set daily limits and open limits on short video and short drama, and turn on the short wait before each open and Take a Break. Use one-tap focus when you want to read or study. If boredom makes you switch rules off, turn on strict mode and give the unlock code to someone at home.

My child cannot play outside on rainy days and stays on the tablet all afternoon. What can I set?

Have a few indoor activities ready and agree how long the tablet can be used. Then on the child's Android tablet or old phone, set daily limits or 0 minutes on games and short video, turn on Take a Break and add a bedtime schedule. Parents keep the unlock code and turn on Anti-Uninstall.

Can it only apply on rainy days?

Ripple does not read the weather. You can add schedules for weekends or evenings, or set daily limits that apply every day.

How is this different from the weekend page?

The weekend page covers whole weekends and holidays spent scrolling. This page covers days when rain or cold keeps you indoors. See the FAQ for more.

Does the iPhone version have these features?

iPhone is at v0.1.5. It has schedules, the block on deleting apps, clock change protection and an unlock code. It does not have daily limits, open limits, 0 minutes, Take a Break, one-tap focus, strict mode, Channels allowance or website limits.

Does it cost money or need an account?

It is free, has no ads, and needs no account.
