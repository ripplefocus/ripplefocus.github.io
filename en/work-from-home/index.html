Scrolling while working from home, and kids home alone on their phone all day

When you work from home there are no coworkers around and the phone sits right next to the laptop. You reply to one work message, open TikTok out of habit, and half an hour is gone, so the work slides into the evening. Kids at home during the holidays do the same. You leave for work in the morning, come back in the evening, and find out they have been on the phone since they woke up and barely touched their homework. Ripple lets you set up work hours and your child's day in advance, so the apps you chose stay closed at those times and daily limits apply even when nobody is home to check. The first part is for adults working from home and the second part is for parents who are at work while their child is home. Android is on v0.1.18 and iPhone on v0.1.5, with different features, so they are described separately.

Adults: keep office rules at home

Add a schedule for work hours

For example add 9:00 to 12:00 and 13:30 to 18:00 on weekdays and put short video and social apps on the list, so they close during those hours. Leave WeChat, Slack, Teams or other work apps off the list.

Keep WeChat chat, limit Moments and Channels

Working from home usually means WeChat stays open. Since Android v0.1.9 Moments and Channels can get a few minutes first, then you write a reason to get 5 more minutes, up to 3 times a day. Chat is not affected. See the Moments allowance page.

A cap for lunch and after work

Outside work hours it is just as easy to keep scrolling. On Android give those apps a daily limit in minutes, for example 40 minutes a day, after which they stay closed for the day. Turn on the pause before opening as well, so you first see how many times you opened the app today and how long you used it.

Lock a few hours when you have a deadline

For the afternoon before a deadline you can turn on Complete Lock for that schedule. Only essential system apps like phone, SMS and alarm work, and the unlock code cannot end it. Skip it for hours when you need to answer messages. For a short burst of focus use Quick Focus.

If you use an iPhone

iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps by schedule, for example TikTok and Xiaohongshu during weekday work hours. It can also block deleting apps, protect against clock changes and use an unlock code. It has no daily limits, open limits, pause before opening, Quick Focus, Complete Lock or strict mode.

Parents: you are at work, your child is home alone

Plan the day together first

Before the holidays sit down with your child and agree on when to get up, which homework to do in the morning, when they can play and for how long. Put that into Ripple afterwards. Rules you agreed on together cause fewer arguments in the evening.

Add schedules that match the day

On your child's Android phone or tablet add schedules, for example 9:00 to 11:30 and 14:30 to 16:30 for homework and 22:00 to 7:00 for sleep. Games and short video apps close then. For online classes use a normal schedule and put the class app on the allowed list. See the homework page.

Set a number for play time

Give games and short video apps a daily limit, for example 60 minutes a day during the holidays, after which they stay closed for the day. You can also give TikTok or Kuaishou a daily open limit. The rules count whether you are home or not.

The child cannot change it

On Android turn on strict mode. Rules can then only be loosened with the unlock code, which in strict mode is at least 8 characters, works at most 3 times a day and stays with the parent. Strict mode turns on Anti-Uninstall too, so the child cannot remove Ripple. Ripple on Android counts time since boot, so changing the system clock does not add time.

Keep alive settings on Chinese brand phones

Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo, Huawei and Honor phones may stop Ripple in the background, which matters more when the child is home alone. After installing, follow the keep alive guide to set autostart and battery optimization.

If the child uses an iPhone or iPad

iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps by schedule, so those apps do not open during homework and sleep time. It can also block deleting apps, protect against clock changes and use an unlock code. It has no daily limits or open limits. See the parental control page.

Setup steps

Install Ripple

On Android download the latest APK and turn on the accessibility service when asked. On iPhone install v0.1.5, which needs iOS 16 or later.

Pick the apps that pull you away

Put short video, games, Xiaohongshu and Weibo on the list. Leave work chat apps, online class apps, maps and payment apps off.

Add schedules for work hours or the child's day

Adults add weekday work hours, kids get homework and sleep schedules. On Android also set daily limits, open limits and the pause before opening for those apps.

Hand over the unlock code

On Android turn on strict mode and Anti-Uninstall. Give your own code to a family member or friend, and keep the code for your child's phone yourself.

FAQ

I keep scrolling my phone while working from home. What can I do?

Add a Ripple schedule for weekday work hours and put short video and social apps on the list. On Android also set a daily limit and the pause before opening, and turn on Complete Lock for deadline hours.

I need WeChat for work. Can I limit only Moments?

Yes. Since Android v0.1.9 Moments and Channels can get a few minutes first, then a written reason gives 5 more minutes, up to 3 times a day. In strict mode the unlock code is used instead, and the time counts toward WeChat's daily limit. Chat is not affected.

I am at work and my child is home alone on the phone during the holidays. How do I manage it?

Agree on a daily plan with your child, then add homework and sleep schedules on their Android phone and give games and short video apps a daily limit. Turn on strict mode and Anti-Uninstall and keep the unlock code yourself.

Will online classes be blocked?

Put the class app on the allowed list and it keeps working during normal schedules. Do not turn on Complete Lock for class hours, because during Complete Lock apps on the allowed list are blocked too.

Does this work on iPhone?

iPhone v0.1.5 blocks selected apps by schedule, can block deleting apps, protects against clock changes and has an unlock code. It has no daily limits, open limits, Complete Lock or strict mode.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

It is free with no ads and no account. More in the FAQ.
