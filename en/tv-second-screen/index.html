Less phone scrolling while the TV is on

After dinner the TV goes on for a series or a match, and the phone stays in your hand with short videos and short dramas. The episode ends, you cannot say what happened, and the TV has just been running. Kids do the same: cartoons play on the TV while they hold a tablet or an old family phone playing games or watching videos, looking at two screens at once. The site also has a binge watching page for watching episode after episode on the phone, and family time and after dinner pages for evenings at home. This page covers the time when the TV and the phone are both on. To be clear, Ripple only manages apps on the phones and tablets it is installed on. It cannot control the TV, a TV box or apps on the TV, and it does not manage playback after you cast from the phone to the TV. What it can do is make the screen in your hand pull less of your attention. The first part below is for adults limiting their own phone, the second is for parents setting up a child's tablet or old phone. Android is at v0.1.18 and iPhone at v0.1.5, and the features differ, so both are covered.

Adults: put the phone down while the TV is on

Set daily limits and open limits on short video and short drama

What people scroll during TV is mostly short video and short drama. Set how long each can run per day, in custom minutes, or 0 minutes to block it all day. Add how many times it can open, and turn on the short wait before each open, so you pause when the show slows down and your hand reaches for the phone. See the short drama page and the open limit page.

Block by schedule during your evening TV time

Many people watch TV at about the same time each day, for example 8 to 10 pm. Add a schedule for it with only short video, short drama and games on the list. Leave WeChat messages, calls and alarms off it, so family can still reach you. For watching episode after episode on the phone, see the binge watching page.

Start one-tap focus before the episode or match

When the time is not fixed, tap one-tap focus on Android as the show starts and pick a length that fits, for example 2 hours for a football match. Choose Lock only the apps I pick and lock short video and games, so score or chat apps you leave unpicked keep working. See the one-tap focus page.

WeChat Channels and Moments can start with a few minutes

On Android you can limit only Channels and Moments inside WeChat: start with a short allowance, then write a reason to extend, up to 3 times a day, counted toward the WeChat daily limit. In strict mode, extending needs the unlock code. WeChat chats and voice calls still work.

Turn on strict mode if you keep switching rules off

If you set rules and then turn them off during the ad break, turn on strict mode. Give an unlock code of at least 8 digits to family, and loosening rules then needs their code. An unlock code can succeed at most 3 times a day. Strict mode also turns on Anti-Uninstall. If you forget the code, you can request release after 72 hours. See the strict mode page.

Parents: a child with a tablet or old phone during cartoons

Set daily limits on games and short video on the tablet or old phone, or 0 minutes

What kids play during TV is mostly games and short video. On the Android tablet or old phone they use, give those apps a daily limit so they stop opening when time is up. A game that is especially hard to stop can be set to 0 minutes to block it all day, then opened up on weekends. See the mobile games page.

Block TV time by schedule

Cartoon time is usually fixed too, for example 7 to 8 pm or weekend mornings. Add that schedule on the tablet or old phone with games and short video on the list, so the screen in their hand is locked while the TV is on. Leave study apps off the list.

Split screen, floating windows and picture-in-picture are blocked too

Older kids drag a video into a floating window or picture-in-picture and keep watching. On Android, listed apps are blocked in split screen, floating windows and picture-in-picture as well, and that time counts toward today's usage. On OPPO a floating window is covered and muted, and the window itself still has to be closed by hand.

The TV needs its own rules

Ripple cannot manage the TV, a TV box or apps on the TV. For TV time, use the TV's own kids mode or a family rule about when it goes off. Ripple covers the tablet and phone in the child's hand, and together the child is not watching two screens at once. See the parental control page.

If the child uses an iPhone

iPhone v0.1.5 can block apps by schedule, block deleting apps, protect against clock changes and use an unlock code, so TV time schedules work. Daily limits, 0 minutes, open limits, one-tap focus, strict mode, Channels allowance and website blocking are Android only for now. On Android remember to turn on Anti-Uninstall. Ripple counts time since the phone started, so moving the clock back does not help.

Setup steps (Android v0.1.18)

Install or update to the latest version

Download the latest Android APK and turn on accessibility when asked. If you have an older build, update in Settings › About › Check for updates. On iPhone install v0.1.5, which needs iOS 16 or later.

Adult phone: daily limits, open limits and an evening TV schedule on short video and short drama

Leave WeChat messages, calls and alarms off the list. If the time is not fixed, use one-tap focus when the show starts.

Child's tablet or old phone: daily limits or 0 minutes on games and short video, plus a TV time schedule

Leave study apps off the list. Manage the TV itself with its own settings or a family rule.

Set unlock codes and Anti-Uninstall on the child's device

Give your own code to family, and keep the code on the child's device with parents. Turn on Anti-Uninstall on Android, with strict mode if you want a firmer lock. On iPhone turn on the block on deleting apps.

FAQ

I keep scrolling short video while a show is on TV. What helps?

On Ripple for Android v0.1.18, set daily limits and open limits on short video and short drama, and add a schedule for your evening TV time so those apps will not open then. If the time is not fixed, start one-tap focus when the show begins. WeChat messages and calls still work. For a firmer rule, turn on strict mode and give the unlock code to family.

My child plays games on a tablet while cartoons are on TV. What can I set?

On the child's Android tablet or old phone, give games and short video a daily limit or set a game to 0 minutes, and add a schedule for TV time so games will not open then. Keep the unlock code with parents and turn on Anti-Uninstall. Ripple cannot manage the TV itself, so use the TV's own settings or a family rule.

Can Ripple limit the TV or a TV box?

No. Ripple only manages apps on the Android phones, Android tablets and iPhones it is installed on. The TV, TV boxes, apps on the TV and playback cast to the TV are outside it.

How is this different from the binge watching, family time and after dinner pages?

The binge watching page covers watching episode after episode on the phone, family time covers evenings with family after work, and after dinner covers scrolling from dinner to bed. This page covers the time when the TV and the phone are both on. More answers are on the FAQ.

Does the iPhone version have these features?

iPhone v0.1.5 has schedules, the block on deleting apps, clock change protection and an unlock code. Daily limits, open limits, 0 minutes, one-tap focus, strict mode, Channels allowance and website blocking are Android only.

Does it cost money or need an account?

It is free with no ads and no account.
