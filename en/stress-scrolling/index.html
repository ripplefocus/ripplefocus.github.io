Less scrolling when you feel down or stressed

Your boss had a go at you, or you argued with someone at home, and in the evening you lie on the sofa scrolling short videos and reading the comments under trending posts. It makes you feel worse and you still keep going, and when you put the phone down it is past one. Kids do this too. After a bad test or a falling out with a friend at school, they come home, say nothing, and stay with the tablet watching videos and playing games, and do not come out for dinner. Reaching for the phone to take your mind off things is normal. The trouble is when it runs for hours and the next day is harder. Ripple cannot fix a bad mood. What it can do is draw a line on short video, short drama, trending news and games, so they close at that point and there is a place to stop. The site has a bedtime page and a trending news page. This page is about scrolling that will not stop when you feel upset or stressed. The first part below is for adults limiting their own phone, the second is for parents setting up a child's tablet or phone. Android is at v0.1.19 and iPhone at v0.1.5, and the features differ, so both are covered.

Adults: a little less scrolling on bad days

Set the rules on a good day

It is hard to set limits while you are upset, so do it when you feel fine. Give short video, short drama and trending news apps a daily limit in custom minutes, for example 40 minutes a day. When it runs out they will not open. See the short drama page.

Add a short wait before each open and an open limit

When you are upset your hand often reaches for the phone before you think. Turn on the short wait before each open for these apps and set how many times a day they can open. In those few seconds you sometimes notice you do not really want to watch. See the open limit page.

Add a night schedule

Scrolling until midnight makes the next day worse. Add a schedule from 11 pm to 7 am with short video, short drama, trending news and games on the list. They will not open during it and come back in the morning. Leave calls, WeChat chats and the alarm off the list, so you can still reach someone to talk. See the bedtime page.

WeChat Channels and Moments can start with a few minutes

On Android you can limit only Channels and Moments inside WeChat: start with a short allowance, then write a reason to extend, up to 3 times a day, counted toward the WeChat daily limit. Writing the reason gives you a moment to decide whether to keep going. In strict mode, extending needs the unlock code.

Turn on strict mode if you switch rules off when upset

Switching the rules off is the easiest thing to do when you feel bad. With strict mode on, loosening the rules needs the unlock code. Give an unlock code of at least 8 digits to family or a friend. The code can succeed at most 3 times a day. Strict mode turns on Anti-Uninstall too, and a lost code can be removed after a 72 hour wait. See the strict mode page.

Parents: a child who will not put the tablet down after a bad day

Talk first, then set the rules

Taking the tablet away the moment a child comes home after a bad test or a hard day often ends in a fight. Ask what happened first, then agree together on how long they can play each day, and set that time in Ripple, so the rule does not need an argument every evening.

Set daily limits on the child's tablet or old phone

On the Android tablet or old phone your child uses, give games and short video a daily limit. When it runs out they will not open. A game that is especially hard to put down right now can be set to 0 minutes for a while and changed back later. See the mobile games page.

Add a night schedule so bedtime holds

An upset child is more likely to stay up watching. Add a night schedule on the child's device with games and short video on the list. Keep calls, WeChat and the apps used to reach parents off the list, so the child can always reach a parent or a friend.

Split screen, floating windows and clock changes

On Android, listed apps are blocked in split screen, floating windows and picture-in-picture. On OPPO phones a floating window can only be covered and muted and has to be closed by hand. Ripple for Android counts time since the phone started, so moving the clock forward does not add time. See the clock change page.

Parents keep the unlock code; iPhone

Only parents know the unlock code. Turn on Anti-Uninstall on Android so the child cannot delete Ripple. iPhone v0.1.5 has schedules, the block on deleting apps, clock change protection and an unlock code, so the night schedule works there too. Daily limits, 0 minutes, open limits, strict mode and Channels allowance are Android only for now. See the parental control page.

Setup steps (Android v0.1.19)

Download the latest Android APK and turn on the Accessibility service as guided. If you have an older version, update in Settings › About › Check for updates. On iPhone install v0.1.5, which needs iOS 16 or later.

Child's tablet or old phone: agree with the child first, then set daily limits or 0 minutes on games and short video and add a night schedule

FAQ

When I feel down I scroll short videos until midnight. What can I set?

On Ripple for Android v0.1.19, set daily limits and open limits on short video, short drama and trending news on a good day, turn on the short wait before each open, and add a night schedule. If you switch rules off when upset, turn on strict mode and give the unlock code to family or a friend. Keep calls and WeChat chats off the list.

My child did badly on a test and will not put the tablet down. What can I set?

Talk with your child first and agree how long they can play each day. Then on the child's Android tablet or old phone, set daily limits on games and short video, or 0 minutes for now, and add a night schedule. Split screen, floating windows and picture-in-picture are blocked too. Parents keep the unlock code, turn on Anti-Uninstall, and clock changes do not help.

Can Ripple make me feel better?

No. Ripple only manages apps on phones and tablets, and what it can do is close short video, trending news and games at set times. If low mood or anxiety lasts a long time and affects eating, sleep, work or school, please talk to family or friends, or get help from a doctor or a school counselor.

How is this different from the bedtime and trending news pages?

The bedtime page covers scrolling before sleep and the trending news page covers checking news all the time. This page covers scrolling that will not stop when you feel upset or stressed. See the FAQ for more.

Does the iPhone version have these features?

iPhone is at v0.1.5. It has schedules, the block on deleting apps, clock change protection and an unlock code. It does not have daily limits, open limits, 0 minutes, one-tap focus, strict mode, Channels allowance or website limits.

Does it cost money or need an account?

It is free, has no ads, and needs no account.
