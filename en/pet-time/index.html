You scroll the whole dog walk, and kids promised to care for the pet but stay on the tablet

You take the dog out in the evening and spend the walk looking at short videos while the dog tugs at the leash. Back home the cat jumps on the sofa and you pet it with one hand while the other keeps scrolling. Your child promised to feed the cat, refill the water and play with the dog every day, and when the time comes they are still watching cartoons on the tablet. This page covers adults setting this up for themselves first, then parents setting it up for their kids. Android is on v0.1.19 and iPhone on v0.1.5. They have different features, so they are covered separately.

Adults: lock the apps you scroll before the walk

Turn on Quick Focus before you head out

On Android open Quick Focus, choose lock only the apps I select, add short video, social and shopping apps, and set it for about as long as your usual walk, such as 40 minutes. It lifts when the time is up.

Keep calls, maps and the camera off the list

On a walk you may need to take a call or check a map, and you will want a photo when the dog is having fun. Leave those apps off the list and they keep working. Looking up more also makes crossing the road safer.

For a regular walk time, add a schedule

Schedules can be set by day of the week. If you walk the dog every evening from 7:00 to 7:40, create a daily schedule and add the apps you keep opening. If weekend walks are longer, add a second schedule that ticks only Saturday and Sunday.

At home with the pet, use daily limits

If you meant to play with the cat or dog in the evening and ended up scrolling, give your most used apps a daily limit in minutes, from 1 minute to 12 hours. Once it is used up the app stays closed for the day. For an app you cannot quit, set 0 minutes and it stays closed all day.

Hand the unlock code to someone at home

With strict mode on, the only way to loosen rules is the unlock code. In strict mode the code is at least 8 characters and works at most 3 times a day, so a partner or roommate can keep it. Strict mode also turns on Anti-Uninstall. If you forget the code you can request removal, which takes effect after 72 hours.

Adults on iPhone

iPhone v0.1.5 has no Quick Focus. Create a schedule that repeats every day, such as 7:00 to 7:40 pm, and select short video and social apps. The system enforces it even if Ripple is closed. It can also block deleting apps, protect against clock changes and use an unlock code. It has no daily limits, 0 minute limits or strict mode.

Kids: the feeding and dog walking they agreed to

Write down the pet jobs first

Agree with your child on the daily jobs, such as filling the food bowl, changing the water and scooping the litter after school, then playing with the dog before dinner. During that time games and cartoons on the tablet will not open, and afterwards they get the screen time you normally agree on.

Set a schedule on the child's device

On the child's Android phone or tablet, create a schedule such as every day from 5:30 to 6:00 pm and add games, short video and cartoon apps. Keep the phone app and the app they use to reach you off the list.

Walking the dog together

If you walk the dog with your child, keep the schedule on for their device too and watch what the dog is up to together. Lock your own scrolling apps with the steps above as well, see parents going first.

Hold tablet games with a daily limit

If your child gets some tablet time each day, give that game a daily limit such as 30 minutes. Once it is used up, the app stays closed for the rest of the day. Set 0 minutes for a game you want gone completely.

Kids cannot turn it off

On Android, add strict mode and Anti-Uninstall and keep the unlock code yourself. Your child cannot turn off the rules or delete Ripple. Android times rules from device uptime, so changing the clock does not help.

Kids on iPhone or iPad

iPhone v0.1.5 can block apps by schedule. Create a daily 5:30 to 6:00 pm schedule so games and short videos do not open. It can also block deleting apps, protect against clock changes and use an unlock code. It has no daily limits, Quick Focus or strict mode. Ripple has no remote management, so set it up directly on the child's device. On Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo, Huawei and Honor phones, follow the keep-alive guide for auto-start and battery settings.

Setup steps

Decide which apps you need with the pet

Leave calls, maps, the camera and the app kids use to reach you off the list. Add only the apps you keep scrolling, such as short video, games and social.

Install Ripple

On Android, download the latest APK and turn on the accessibility service as prompted. On iPhone, install v0.1.5, which needs iOS 16 or later.

Set it before you start

For a one-off walk on Android use Quick Focus. For a fixed walking or feeding time, create a repeating schedule. On the child's device add strict mode and Anti-Uninstall. On iPhone create a daily schedule.

When you are back

Scroll afterwards if you like, and hold everyday use with daily limits if you scroll a lot. For kids, see reward time and chores.

FAQ

I need calls and maps on the walk. Will they be locked?

Not unless they are on the list. With Quick Focus set to lock only the apps I select, only the apps you add are locked.

I want to post a photo of the dog. What then?

The camera stays open, so take the photo now and post it when the session ends. If you added your messaging app to the list, it will not open during the walk.

My walk time changes every day. Do I need a schedule?

On Android use Quick Focus before you leave. It lifts on time. Add a schedule only for times that are fixed.

My child wants the tablet as soon as the cat is fed. What then?

Give the game a daily limit such as 30 minutes, so it closes for the day once used up. Add strict mode and keep the unlock code yourself.

Can I set it up remotely from my own phone?

Not at the moment. Ripple settings stay on the phone they are set on. There is no remote management, so set it up directly on your child's device.

Does it work on iPhone?

iPhone v0.1.5 can block selected apps by schedule, block deleting apps, protect against clock changes and use an unlock code. It has no daily limits, 0 minute limits, Quick Focus or strict mode. Create a schedule that repeats every day.

Does it cost anything? Do I need an account?

It is free, with no ads and no account. See the FAQ for more.
