When to Choose Each Tool

Choose ScreenZen or One Sec if…

You only need a 10-second breathing pause before opening Instagram or Reddit, and you do not struggle with going into Android Settings to disable or uninstall the blocker late at night.

Choose Ripple if…

You need a hard daily cap and sleep schedule where your midnight self is forced to wait 15 minutes to 2 hours (or ask a friend/parent for a PIN) before relaxing any rule or uninstalling, and you want optional on-device wireless debugging or Shizuku auto-healing without paying a yearly subscription.
