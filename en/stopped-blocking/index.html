App limits suddenly stopped working: what to check

You set a limit, it worked for a few days, and then one evening the app opens as if nothing was set. Adults notice it when they catch themselves scrolling past their own cutoff. Parents usually find out from the child. I make Ripple, a free app blocker with no account, so the examples use Ripple. Most of the checks also apply to other blockers. Ripple for Android is v0.1.30 and Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5.

Common reasons

The accessibility service was turned off

On Android, Ripple sees which app is open through an accessibility service. If the system stops it, nothing gets blocked. Many phones stop it after you swipe the app away from recent apps, after a long night in standby, or after a crash.

Battery saving froze the app

Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo, Honor and Huawei phones are strict about background apps. If Ripple is not allowed to run in the background, timers and limits can stop until you open it again.

The rule itself changed

A schedule may have ended, a daily amount may have been raised, or an app may have been added to the list of apps that are always allowed. In Ripple, loosening a rule usually needs a 15 minute wait or the unlock code, so check whether someone waited it out.

An old version or a page the app could not see

Each release fixes cases where a page was not seen. For example, on some phones WeChat hides Channels opened from an Official Account article from accessibility services. Android v0.1.30 also reads usage records to catch this, but that fix has so far been tested only on an emulator.

On your own phone

Check the switch first

Open Settings, then Accessibility, and see whether Ripple is still on. If it keeps turning off, follow the battery and background steps for your phone brand in the keep alive guide.

Make it harder for yourself to turn it off

Turn on uninstall protection. If you are the one who keeps waiting out the 15 minutes, turn on strict mode, where rules loosen only with the unlock code, and give the code to a friend or family member. Strict mode is on Android only.

Look at the usage calendar

The calendar on the Today page shows how long each app was used and how many times it was blocked on each day. A day with use but no blocks points to the day the limit stopped working.

iPhone

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps on a schedule through Screen Time. Check that Ripple still has Screen Time permission and that the schedule is on. Daily amounts, strict mode and the usage calendar are not on iPhone yet.

On a child's phone

Ask what happened, then check

Children find gaps quickly. Changing the clock does not help with Ripple, because its timers use time since the phone started. The usual gaps are a turned off service, a frozen app or a rule that was loosened with the code.

Keep the code with you

On a child's phone, set the unlock code yourself and do not type it where the child can watch. Turn on uninstall protection, so removing Ripple needs the wait or the code.

Send us the diagnostic log

From v0.1.30, Settings › About has Export Diagnostic Log. It records page names, switch and permission states and the steps of blocking and timing. It has no chat content and does not read text on screen. If blocking stopped and you cannot see why, email it to dzytmk@qq.com.

What Ripple cannot do

Ripple has no remote management. You set rules on the child's phone itself, and you cannot see or change them from your own phone. On iPhone it only blocks by schedule.

Step by step

1. Update

Install the latest version from the official site or GitHub. The signing certificate is the same, so your rules stay.

2. Check the accessibility switch (Android)

If it is off, turn it back on. On Android 13 or later you may first need to allow restricted settings for Ripple.

3. Allow background running

Set battery use for Ripple to unrestricted and lock its card in recent apps, using the steps for your brand.

4. Read the rules again

Look at schedules, daily amounts and the always allowed list. A rule that was loosened shows its new value.

5. Export the log and send it

If it still does not block, export the diagnostic log from Settings › About and email it to us.

FAQ

Why does the accessibility service keep turning off?

Usually because of the phone's battery saving or because the app was swiped away. Ripple can restore it a few seconds after being stopped if you turn on the on-device wireless debugging guard. The keep alive guide lists steps for each brand.

Can my child turn Ripple off?

Turning off a rule needs the 15 minute wait or the unlock code. In strict mode only the code works. With uninstall protection on, removing Ripple needs the wait or the code too. Keep the code yourself.

Does the diagnostic log upload anything by itself?

No. It stays on the phone until you export it and choose where to send it.

Is the WeChat Channels article fix confirmed?

Not yet. In v0.1.30 it was tested on an emulator with a simulated page and not on a real phone with real WeChat. Videos that play inside an article are not blocked yet.
