Living alone, or parenting alone, and the phone

When you live alone nobody sees how long you have been on the phone. You get home, eat with a video on, and it is past one before you put it down. A whole weekend can go the same way. Parents who look after a child mostly on their own have a different problem. The phone is the only way to get twenty quiet minutes to cook, and then the child does not want to give it back. I make Ripple, a free app blocker. Ripple for Android is v0.1.20 and Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5. The first part is for your own phone and the second part is for a child's phone.

If you live alone

Calls and chats stay open

Living alone, you want people to be able to reach you. Ripple only blocks the apps you add. Calls, SMS and alarms are never blocked, and WeChat can stay open with only Channels or Moments blocked.

A night schedule

A schedule such as 23:30 to 7:00 closes the video and game apps for the night. The phone still works as a phone and an alarm.

A daily amount for the evening

Give the app that takes your evening a daily limit, for example 60 minutes. Once it is used up the app shows the block screen until the next day. An open limit helps with apps you keep opening for a quick look.

Weekends at home

Add a weekend schedule for part of the day, such as 10:00 to 13:00, so at least the morning is yours. If you decide to go out or read on the spot, the quick focus button on the Today page locks the listed apps right away.

Someone to hold the code

Without strict mode, loosening a rule needs a 15 minute wait (30 minutes for apps like Douyin) or the unlock code. With strict mode only the code works, at most 3 successful uses a day. Ask a friend or family member to set the code and keep it.

If you use an iPhone

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps by schedule, can stop apps being deleted, protects against clock changes and uses an unlock code. It has no daily limits or open limits.

If you look after a child mostly by yourself

Using the phone to get a break is normal

When you are the only adult at home, handing over the phone so you can cook or shower happens. The rules below are for keeping that from stretching into the whole evening.

A daily amount per app

On Android give the cartoon, video and game apps a daily limit, such as 40 minutes each. When it runs out you do not have to be the one who says no, the block screen does it.

Dinner and bedtime

Add a schedule for dinner and one for bedtime, such as 20:30 to 7:00. Calls keep working, so the child can still call you.

Rules you cannot argue about

Turn on strict mode and keep the code to yourself. Anti-Uninstall turns on with strict mode and needs Android 11 or later. Changing the clock does not end a schedule, and a forgotten code is lifted 72 hours after a request.

Your own phone after bedtime

Once the child is asleep, the evening is often the only time you have, and it goes on scrolling. A night schedule on your own phone, such as 23:30 to 7:00, gets you to bed.

Child on iPhone or iPad

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 can block apps by schedule, which covers dinner and bedtime. For daily amounts use Screen Time with Family Sharing.

Setup steps

Install

On Android download the latest APK and turn on the accessibility service when asked. Ripple needs Android 8.0 or later. If the phone cleans up background apps, follow the keep alive guide.

Pick the apps

Add short video, short drama, live streaming and game apps. Leave out calls, chats, maps, payment and delivery apps.

Set the times

One night schedule, then daily limits for the one or two apps that take the most time.

Test it

Open a listed app and check that the block screen shows. Write the unlock code down and give it to the person who keeps it.

Look back after a week

The usage calendar on Android shows how much time each app took every day, so you can see whether the evenings got shorter.

FAQ

Will Ripple block calls or messages?

No. It only blocks apps you add to a schedule or limit. Calls, SMS and alarms are never blocked.

Can I block only WeChat Channels and keep chats?

Yes on Android. Ripple can block only Channels or Moments and leave chats and calls alone.

Can I change my child's rules from my own phone?

No. Ripple has no remote management. Rules are set on the child's phone.

What does Ripple for iPhone do?

Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 blocks apps by schedule, can stop apps being deleted, protects against clock changes and uses an unlock code. It has no daily limits.

Does it cost anything?

Ripple is free, with no ads and no account. More in the FAQ.
