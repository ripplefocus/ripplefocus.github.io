FAQ

Can I block short videos but still watch lectures on YouTube?

Yes. Ripple can block only the YouTube Shorts screen, so normal YouTube videos, lecture playlists and course apps keep working.

What stops me from turning the blocker off when I get bored?

Loosening or removing any rule starts a 15 minute cooldown first. You can also give the unlock code to a roommate, study partner or parent, so you have no instant way out.

Can it block my phone late at night automatically?

Yes. Add a schedule such as 11 pm to 7 am for short video and game apps. Timers count time since boot, so changing the system clock does not end them early.

Can I bypass it by uninstalling or force stopping?

On Android 11 and later, turning on the on-device wireless debugging guard locks the uninstall button and brings Ripple back about 2.5 seconds after it is swiped away or force stopped. No computer or root is needed.

Can parents set it up for a high school student?

Yes. Set the rules on the student's phone and keep the unlock code yourself. Chat apps can stay open while only Shorts, Reels or WeChat Channels are blocked.
