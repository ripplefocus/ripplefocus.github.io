Why changing the clock defeats many limits

When a limit is checked against the system clock, such as "unlock at 21:00" or "30 minutes left", the app sees whatever time the system reports. Set the clock to the next morning and the app thinks the limit is over. Ripple counts its cooldowns and focus timers by the time that has actually passed since boot. That counter cannot be edited in Settings, so changing the date, time or time zone does not shorten anything.

FAQ

If my child moves the phone clock forward, does the block end early?

No. Ripple counts cooldowns and focus timers with a monotonic clock, which measures time since the phone booted. Changing the date, time or time zone in Settings does not change the remaining time.

What about turning off automatic time or changing the time zone?

Both only change the time the system displays. Ripple's timers do not read that value, so neither change has any effect.

Does restarting the phone reset the timer?

With the guard turned on, Ripple resumes blocking within about 1 minute after the phone restarts and is unlocked. Rules are stored on the phone itself.

What if my child swipes Ripple away in Recents or force-stops it in Settings?

On Android 11 and later, with the on-device wireless debugging guard turned on, Ripple comes back about 2.5 seconds after being swiped away or force-stopped, and the system uninstall button is locked. Setup happens on the child's phone without a computer.

How do I give my child a break when I want to?

Save an emergency unlock code during setup and keep it yourself. Entering the code lifts the block right away. Without the code, loosening any rule requires waiting through a 15-minute cooldown.
