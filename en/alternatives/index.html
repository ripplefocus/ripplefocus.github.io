Ripple compared with other app blockers and parental controls

I make Ripple, a free app blocker for Android and iPhone with no account. Over the past weeks I wrote one comparison page for each group of apps people ask about. This page links all of them, sorted by what you are trying to do. Each comparison says where the other app is the better pick. Ripple for Android is v0.1.31 and Ripple for iPhone is v0.1.5, and the iPhone version only blocks apps on a schedule.

Start with what your phone already has

Digital Wellbeing, Family Link, iPhone Screen Time

All free. If an app timer on your own phone is enough, or you want to manage a child's phone from your own, try these first.

Teen modes inside TikTok, Instagram, YouTube

Good for one app at a time. Easy to get around by switching to another app or the browser.

Limiting your own phone

one sec and ScreenZen

A pause before an app opens. ScreenZen is free and works on both platforms. Good if a short delay is enough for you.

Opal and Freedom

Polished blockers whose main features need a subscription. Freedom also syncs with your computer.

AppBlock and Stay Focused

Long running Android blockers. Compared on uninstall protection, clock changes and background killing.

FocusMe and BlockerX

For people who keep turning their own limits off. FocusMe syncs with a computer, BlockerX focuses on adult content.

YourHour and Regain

Usage trackers with limits, ads or Pro plans, and Reels blocking.

StayFree and ActionDash

Mostly screen time statistics, with some limits on top.

Flipd and SPACE

Study timers and habit tracking for exam season.

Brick and Unpluq

Blocked apps open only after you tap the phone on an NFC puck or tag. Brick is a $59 puck, the Unpluq tag needs Premium.

Clearspace and Jomo

Clearspace lets you earn minutes with push-ups or a breathing break on iPhone and Android. Jomo blocks until you finish an action and has no Android app.

Olauncher and minimalist phone

Calm home screen launchers. They make apps harder to find but do not block them.

BlockSite and Lock Me Out

Website and keyword blocking, and lockouts based on usage or location.

Zenze

Free Android blocker with ads, scores and leaderboards. The same page covers Mobicip.

TiedSiren, Holdfast

TiedSiren Strict Mode needs a subscription, Holdfast blocks only Reels and Shorts. Both Android only.

Setting limits on a child's phone

Qustodio and Bark

Paid services with reports, web filtering and location, managed from the parent's phone.

Kidslox and FamiSafe

Paid parental control apps with remote management and monitoring.

OurPact and Norton Family

Paid app rules, schedules, web supervision and location.

Mobicip

Paid parental control for iPhone, Android and computers, with remote lock and reports.

Microsoft Family Safety

Free, works best when the family uses Windows and Xbox. No app limits on iPhone.

TimeLimit.io and DetoxDroid

Open source. TimeLimit.io is a parental control on F-Droid, DetoxDroid is a digital detox tool.

Apps popular in China

OffPhone (远离手机) and Forest

Focus session apps that lock the phone for a set time.

WillStone (自律石头)

Earn screen time with reading, vocabulary and steps. On app stores, some features need a membership.

Bu Zuo Shou Ji Kong and Tomato ToDo

A feature heavy Android lock app with SVIP, and a pomodoro timer with study rooms.

What Ripple can and cannot do

Android v0.1.31

Daily limits per app (0 minutes blocks an app all day), schedules, open limits, breaks after continuous use, one tap focus, website limits, strict mode where rules only loosen with an unlock code (3 successful uses a day), and Anti-Uninstall on Android 11 or later.

iPhone v0.1.5

Schedules that block apps, protection against deleting apps and changing the time, and an unlock code. No daily limits yet.

What it does not have

No parent app, no remote management, no reports sent to another phone, no location. Rules are set on the phone itself.

How to choose

You only need a timer on your own phone

Use the built in timer first. Move to Ripple when you keep turning it off, and give the unlock code to a partner or friend.

You want to manage a child's phone from your own

Family Link and iPhone Screen Time are free. Qustodio, Mobicip and similar paid services add reports and location.

Your child has an Android phone and you set rules in person

Ripple can block a game all day with a 0 minute limit and keep homework hours free of short videos, with strict mode on and the code kept by you.

Questions

Is Ripple free?

Yes. No ads, no account and nothing to buy in the app.

Can I manage my child's phone remotely with Ripple?

No. Ripple has no remote management. Rules are set on the child's phone, and strict mode stops them from being loosened without the code.

Does the iPhone version have daily limits?

Not yet. Ripple for iPhone v0.1.5 only blocks apps on a schedule.
